## The pattern

- **Gateways** sit *below* the model call and arbitrate transport.
- **Observability/eval** sits *after* the model call and reports.
- **Agent frameworks** sit *around* the model call and choreograph.
- **DSPy** sits *before* the model call but only offline, for developers, not agents.
- **Sandboxes** sit *next to* the model call as a tool.

**Nobody sits *before* the model call, *at runtime*, exposing planning + learning as an agent-facing API.** That is nerve.
